Method
------
  Parent compound SMILES submitted to /predict endpoint.
  Neutral monoisotopic mass from parent metrics in JSON response.
  Literature masses from NIST Chemistry WebBook and PubChem.
  ppm error = (predicted - literature) / literature * 10^6

-------

  All within 1.0 ppm    : 10/10

  Max |mDa error|       : 0.0000 mDa

Interpretation
--------------
  All 10/10 compounds show ppm error < 1.0 ppm.
  The [M+H]+ adduct mass is calculated as:
      [M+H]+ = M_neutral + 1.007276 Da (proton mass, IUPAC 2018)
  Instrument mass accuracy (1-5 ppm on Orbitrap/Q-TOF) is the
  dominant source of error in practice, not this calculation.
